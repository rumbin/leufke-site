The map below depicts the location of "Haus Leufke" in Lenzkirch-Kappel.
It nicely illustrates the beautiful position at the outskirts of the village which is built on a south-facing slope. 
Towards south, there is an unobstructed view towards the Alps.

In continuation of the street "Mühlhaldeweg" you will find a hiking trail which offers an ideal starting point for nature hikes towards Lenzkirch or the famous "Wutachschlucht".

In order to determine the directions, we recommend to choose ``Directions`` link in the map below.
